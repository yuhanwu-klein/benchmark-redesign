# Loop research notes

- 8 interviews (Jun 2026) with people who had quit at least two habit apps.
- v2 prototype test (Sep 2026): 5 participants, unmoderated, one week. No retention data was collected; Loop has not launched.

## Insights
1. **Breaking a streak felt like failing.** 6 of 8 quit an app within a week of losing a long streak. "One bad day and 40 days were gone."
2. **Advice from the app felt like nagging.** 5 of 8 turned off motivational notifications in past apps; in the v2 test, 4 of 5 called the AI coach "preachy".
3. **People wanted to understand, not just count.** 5 of 8 said they never knew why a habit slipped.

## Competitors (checked Sep 2026)
- Streaks (iOS): reminders yes; streak resets on a miss unless you mark a rest day in advance.
- Habitica: reminders yes; missing a daily costs health points (game penalty).
- Finch: reminders yes; self-care pet, reflection prompts.
- Loop's difference: grace days that work after the fact, and a private weekly reflection.
