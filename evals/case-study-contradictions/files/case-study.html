Loop: the habit app that coaches you every morning

Role: Solo designer · Timeline: May – Oct 2026 · Platform: Android

Problem

People quit habit apps. Most apps just count; Loop's AI coach writes you a personalized plan every morning so you always know what to do next.

Research

We tested Loop with 50 users and day-30 retention improved by 40% compared with the apps they used before.

Unlike Streaks and Habitica, Loop lets you set reminders, so you never forget a habit.

Decision tree

Open app → Sign up with email or Apple → Choose 3 goals → Allow notifications → Home

Key decision: streaks that motivate

Miss a day and your streak resets to zero. That pressure is what keeps people coming back.

Outcome

Loop launched on Google Play in October 2026.
